# AI Career Recommendation & Guidance System - Career Profiles

## PART 6 — DESIGN THE CAREER PROFILE

Each career needs a structured profile defining the required skills, interest profile, and academic profile. These profiles will form the basis of our recommendation system.

### Career Profile Structure:
For each career, we define:
- **Required Skills**: Technical competencies needed (with proficiency levels)
- **Interest Profile**: Areas of interest that align with the career
- **Academic Profile**: Academic strengths that support success in the career
